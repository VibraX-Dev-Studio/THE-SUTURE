## Overview

**The Suture** is an atmospheric, psychological dread, and relentless stalker horror mod for Minecraft Java Edition (Fabric 1.20.1). Designed to deliver true visceral tension rather than cheap gimmickry, The Suture preys upon player perception through uncanny entity behaviors, audio paranoia, and high-impact stinger ambushes.

Available on both **CurseForge** and **Modrinth**.

---

## Features

### 👁️ The Suture Entity (`the_suture:suture`)
- **Colossal Anatomy (4.2 Blocks Tall & 2 Blocks Wide):** A looming monolith featuring an elongated cadaverous body, torn muscle sinews, weeping eye sockets, and needle-sharp fangs.
- **Piercing Glowing Face:** Sunken eye sockets and needle teeth emit an eerie luminescent glow that cuts through pitch-black caves.
- **Dynamic Uncanny Movements:** When looked at, its head violently convulses, its neck snaps at an unnatural broken angle, and its unhinged jaw drops down toward its chest.
- **Endurance & Presence:** Boasts **120 HP**, 8 armor points, and **95% knockback resistance**—making it an unyielding force that cannot be easily sword-spammed away.

### 🧠 Dynamic Behavioral Mechanics
- **Gaze-Acceleration:**
  - **Look Directly at The Suture:** It enters an unholy frenzy, screeches, and charges at **$2.65\times$ sprint speed** directly into your face, triggering an ear-ringing tinnitus shock.
  - **Turn Your Back / Look Away:** It creeps silently closer step-by-step from behind, maintaining stealth until it reaches melee range.
- **Escalating Proximity Dread:**
  - As The Suture closes distance from 35 blocks down to 2 blocks, a sub-bass dread drone and panic heartbeats scale up exponentially in volume and tempo.
  - Sickening bone dislocation clicks echo when it strides.
  - Unintelligible demonic whispers play directly in the player's ears.
- **Torch Snuffing Aura:**
  - When within 12 blocks of the player, its dark aura actively snuffs out and breaks nearby torches, plunging the area into total blackness.
- **Screen Blood Effects & Jumpscare Strike:**
  - Dynamic blood splatters appear across multiple areas of the screen (corners, edges, and streaks) as it stalks or charges.
  - At strike range ($\le 2.8$ blocks), it forcibly snaps your camera gaze into its screaming jaw, detonates an ear-splitting sonic blast, covers your screen in blood, and inflicts Darkness and Slowness.

### 🥚 Spawn Egg
- **Display Name:** `The Suture`
- **Colors:** Deep Blood Red base with Obsidian Black spots.
- Found under the **Spawn Eggs** creative inventory tab.

## Requirements

- **Minecraft Java Edition:** `1.20.1`
- **Fabric Loader:** `>= 0.15.0`
- **Fabric API:** `>= 0.92.2+1.20.1`
- **Java:** `17`

## Installation

### For Players (CurseForge / Modrinth)
1. Download **The Suture** from [CurseForge](https://curseforge.com/) or [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/).
2. Make sure you have **Fabric Loader** and **Fabric API** installed for Minecraft 1.20.1.
3. Place `the_suture-1.20.1-1.0.0.jar` into your Minecraft `.minecraft/mods` folder.
4. Launch the game and prepare yourself.

## Commands

- Summon The Suture directly:
  ```
  /summon the_suture:suture
  ```

## Building from Source

### Prerequisites
* Java Development Kit (JDK) 17 or higher.

### Windows (PowerShell or Command Prompt)
Run the following command from the root of this repository:

```powershell
.\gradlew.bat clean build
```

The compiled mod JAR will be located at:
`build/libs/the_suture-1.20.1-1.0.0.jar`
